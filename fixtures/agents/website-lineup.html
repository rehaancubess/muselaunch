Pocket Play turns your Amazon Fire TV into a party arcade. Everyone joins with the phone in their pocket.

Get it on Fire TV

Couch to kick-off in three moves.

  1. 01

    Open it on the TV

    Start Pocket Play. The code to join is waiting on the home screen.

  2. 02

    Everyone scans

    Point a camera at the code, type a name and you're in. Up to six players.

  3. 03

    Pick a game. Play.

    Each phone turns into the right controller for the game, and stays connected all night.

Questions, answered

Which TVs does it work on?

Pocket Play runs on Amazon Fire TV and Fire TV Stick.

Do I need controllers?

No. Every player uses their own phone, iPhone or Android, in the browser.
